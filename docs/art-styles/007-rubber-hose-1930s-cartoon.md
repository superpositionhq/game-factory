## Visual definition

- **Shape language and proportions:** Limbs are boneless tubes of constant thickness with no elbows or knees. Heads and bodies are circles and pears. Hands have four fingers and white gloves; eyes are tall ovals with a pie-cut pupil.
- **Line and outline:** Thick black outline (4 to 6 px) of even weight on every shape, including inner features.
- **Shading model:** Flat fill. At most one soft airbrushed shadow shape on the body.
- **Palette:** `#1a1612` `#f1e3c2` `#c9543a` `#e2a93b` `#6f8f8a` `#8a5a3c` `#fff8e6`. Cream paper, warm black, and faded red, mustard and teal, as if printed on aged film stock.
- **Lighting:** None in the image-making sense. Everything is evenly lit.
- **Texture and surface:** Characters are flat. Backgrounds look like watercolour: soft washes with paper grain.
- **Atmosphere and post-processing:** Film grain, dust specks, vertical scratches, slight flicker, soft blur, vignette and small chromatic fringing.
- **Camera and framing:** Flat, side-on staging like a theatre stage; orthographic or long lens.
- **UI and typography:** Panels look like 1930s film title cards: cream card (`#f1e3c2`) with warm-black lettering and scalloped or ribbon borders. Faces are bouncy hand-drawn display serifs and brush script. Health and counters appear as playing-card tokens or thick outlined numerals in faded red and mustard.
- **VFX and motion:** Effects are drawn frame by frame with the same thick line: puff clouds, star bursts, sweat drops and smoke rings. Animation runs on ones or twos at 24 frames per second, with constant bounce, heavy squash and stretch and looping idle bobs.

## Unreal recipe

### Assets

Build nearly everything. Characters are made in Blender background mode from spheres and pears with tube limbs of constant radius; give each limb 5 or 6 bones with no elbow or knee and animate them as arcs (a spline IK in Blender, baked to FBX). Hands have four fingers and white gloves; eyes are tall ovals with a pie-cut pupil mesh.For backgrounds, take simple stage-like props from packs and replace their textures with watercolour washes generated by script (blurred colour fields multiplied by paper grain). Arrange levels as flat layers side-on to the camera.

### Materials

`M_HoseFlat` is Unlit: one palette colour parameter and an optional soft radial mask that darkens one airbrushed shadow shape on the body. `M_Wash` is Unlit for backgrounds and samples the painted wash texture. The outline is an inverted hull: a Masked two-sided Unlit material in `#1a1612` showing back faces only (TwoSidedSign), pushed along the vertex normal by World Position Offset, scaled with camera distance to hold 4 to 6 px, assigned as Overlay Material. Inner features such as mouths, eye rims and glove seams are separate thin black meshes.

### Lighting and atmosphere

No lighting is used. Turn off Lumen GI and reflections, shadows, fog and Sky Atmosphere. The gameplay camera is orthographic or a long lens (15 to 20 degree field of view) held side-on.

### Post-processing

Tone Curve Amount 0, Manual exposure. Then the film treatment, as one material `PP_OldFilm` at Scene Color After Tonemapping:

- multiply by a cream tint (`#f1e3c2`) and lower saturation about 20 percent
- vertical scratches and dust specks from a scrolling mask texture re-seeded 12 times per second with `Floor(Time * 12)`
- global brightness flicker of plus or minus 3 percent on the same clock
- a 1 to 2 px blur by averaging four offset samples

On the volume: Film Grain Intensity 0.6, Vignette Intensity 0.6, Scene Fringe Intensity 0.5, Bloom Intensity 0, Motion Blur Amount 0. FXAA anti-aliasing.

### UI and effects

Menus are title cards: a cream Image with a scalloped or ribbon border texture and warm-black lettering in a bouncy display serif (Chicle, Rye) with Lobster for script lines. Health shows as playing-card tokens; counters are thick digits with a black font outline in `#c9543a` and `#e2a93b`. Wrap the HUD in a Retainer Box whose effect material applies the same grain and flicker. Niagara effects are sprite flipbooks drawn with the thick line (puff clouds, star bursts, sweat drops, smoke rings), 6 to 8 SubUV frames played at 12 fps.

## Do / Do not

Do:

- Bend limbs as smooth arcs without joints.
- Keep outline width constant and thick.
- Apply grain, scratches and flicker over the whole frame.
- Use white gloves and pie-cut eyes on characters.

Do not:

- Do not use thin lines on clean flat colour without grain; that reads as `011`.
- Do not add cel bands or rim highlights; that reads as `015`.
- Do not go fully greyscale with painted shadows; that reads as `071`.
- Do not use sharp digital edges without film softness.

## Nearest styles

- `011` Clear-line adventure comic: this style has a thick line, boneless limbs and film grain, scratches and flicker over faded colours; `011` has a thin clean line on crisp flat colour with no grain.
- `081` Ben-Day pop art: this style uses faded cream, mustard and teal with soft watercolour backgrounds and no dots; `081` uses pure primaries filled with a regular grid of large dots.
- `022` Sumi-e ink wash: this style has a thick line of even weight on aged-film cream; `022` has calligraphic brush strokes that swell, thin and break, on brighter paper with uneven colour wash.
